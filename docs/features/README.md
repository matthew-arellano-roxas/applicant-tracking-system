## Overview

Recruiting teaching and non-teaching personnel involves staffing requests, approvals, job postings, applicant review, interviews, hiring decisions, and employee records. The ATS centralizes this workflow for applicants, Human Resources (HR), Department Heads, the Dean, and administrators.

## Problem addressed

| Challenge | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant records are separate or maintained manually | Applicant information is difficult to organize and retrieve. |
| Recruitment has multiple stages | It is difficult to see an applicant's current status. |
| Several groups participate in hiring | HR, Department Heads, the Dean, and other staff must coordinate information. |
| There are many applicants | Manual review takes significant time and effort. |
| Recruitment information is not centralized | Staff have difficulty monitoring the overall process. |
| Employee details must be entered again after hiring | Information is duplicated when an applicant becomes an employee. |

## Goals

- Keep staffing requests, job posts, applications, screening information, interviews, and employee records together.
- Support role-based access across the recruitment process.
- Help staff review applicants and track application progress.
- Let applicants browse openings, apply, and check their application status.
- Reduce repeated entry when applicant information is used for employee records.
- Keep the initial screening advisory and final hiring decisions with authorized personnel.

## Recruitment workflow

```text
Staffing request → Dean review → Job post → Application → CV screening
→ Candidate review → Interview and evaluation → Hiring decision
→ Employee record and department assignment
```

1. A Department Head submits a staffing request for their department.
2. The Dean reviews the pending request.
3. HR manages approved requests and publishes job posts.
4. Applicants browse open positions and submit applications with a CV.
5. The system provides an initial CV review to help staff assess applications; it is advisory, not a final hiring decision.
6. Authorized staff review applications and manage their status.
7. Interview details and evaluations are recorded in the system.
8. HR manages employee records and their document checklists.

## Capabilities by role

| Role | Main capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Applicant** | Register and verify an account, browse open positions, upload or replace a PDF CV, apply for jobs, track application status, view interviews, and manage account settings. |
| **Human Resources (HR)** | View recruitment activity, manage hiring requests and published posts, review applications and CV screening status, manage interviews, and maintain employee records and document checklists. |
| **Department Head** | Submit and track department staffing requests, review applicants, and complete assigned interview evaluations. |
| **Dean** | Review, approve, or reject pending staffing requests. |
| **Employee** | View assigned interviews and personal employee document checklist. |
| **Administrator** | Manage users and roles, departments, and other administrative workspace functions. |

Available pages are guarded by role. See the [page guide](../pages/README.md) for routes and descriptions of each screen.
